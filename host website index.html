About Janakpur

A city rich in history, religion, art and Mithila culture.

The City of Janaki

Janakpur is one of the most important cultural and religious cities of Nepal. It is famous for the magnificent Janaki Mandir and its connection with the ancient Mithila Kingdom.

The city is also known for Mithila art, colorful festivals, traditional culture and beautiful ponds.

Whether you love history, architecture, religion or local culture, Janakpur offers something interesting to discover.

Places to Visit

Explore some of the famous attractions of Janakpur.

Janaki Mandir

The magnificent Janaki Mandir is one of the most famous landmarks of Janakpur and an important religious site.

Ram Mandir

Visit the historic Ram Mandir and experience the spiritual atmosphere of the city.

Mithila Culture

Experience traditional Mithila art, culture, festivals and colorful local traditions.

Ganga Sagar

Ganga Sagar is a popular pond located near the heart of Janakpur and is an important local landmark.

Dhanush Sagar

Dhanush Sagar is another famous pond and a peaceful place to visit while exploring Janakpur.

Mithila Art

Discover the beautiful traditional paintings and artistic heritage of the Mithila region.
